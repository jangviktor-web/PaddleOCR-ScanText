A powerful offline OCR (Optical Character Recognition) app for Android, powered by PaddleOCR PP-OCRv5 engine. Supports Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, and Traditional Chinese text recognition — all processing happens locally on your device, no internet connection required.

Features:
* Fully offline — all recognition runs locally, protecting your privacy
* PP-OCRv5 engine — latest generation OCR model with high accuracy
* Multi-language support — Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Traditional Chinese
* Three interaction modes — Box selection, line-by-line, word segmentation
* Smart Chinese word segmentation — integrated Jieba segmentation engine
* History management — auto-saves recognition history
